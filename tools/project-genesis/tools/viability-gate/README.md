# Viability Gate

Status: `INCUBATION / DOGFOODING`

## Purpose

Determine whether a raw idea deserves to become a project and what the smallest admissible proprietary residual is.

The tool must attack the idea before legitimizing it.

## Mandatory order

```text
UNDERSTAND INTENT
  ↓
SEARCH FOR COMPLETE ADOPTION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR SUPPORTED CUSTOMIZATION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR COMPOSITION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR ADAPTATION
  ↓
IDENTIFY ONLY THE PROVEN RESIDUAL
  ↓
ASSESS ENGINEERING / BUSINESS / RESEARCH RELEVANCE
  ↓
VERDICT
```

## Verdicts

- `NO-GO`
- `GO-BY-ADOPTION`
- `GO-CUSTOMIZE`
- `GO-COMPOSE-ADAPT`
- `GO-BUILD-MINIMUM-RESIDUAL`

## Required evidence

At minimum, when applicable:

- raw idea and corrected boundary;
- candidate universe;
- complete-product alternatives;
- partial capability alternatives;
- composition attempts;
- customization options;
- adaptation options;
- technical feasibility;
- maintenance burden;
- one-human-company sustainability;
- licensing and operational risks;
- market evidence when commercial viability is claimed;
- academic prior art and research-frontier classification when scientific relevance is claimed;
- residual definition;
- uncertainty and blind spots;
- search saturation evidence.

## Separation of verdict dimensions

Engineering, business and research must be evaluated separately before final reconciliation.

A commercial GO does not establish academic novelty.
An academic contribution does not establish a viable product.
A technically feasible build does not justify proprietary ownership when adoption dominates.

## Completion

Naming is forbidden until the viability verdict establishes that a project identity is warranted.
