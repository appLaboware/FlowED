# Viability Gate

Status: `INCUBATION / DOGFOODING`

## Purpose

Position a raw idea against available evidence and determine, as far as the investigation supports, what already exists, what can be reused or transformed, and what residual still requires owned factorization.

The tool does **not** judge whether an idea is good or bad and does not decide for the human.

> **Not a verdict. A position.**

The historical directory name `viability-gate` is retained during incubation. Its semantic role is broader: evidence positioning, sourcing discovery, exclusivity characterization and residual-factorization analysis.

## PMFN orientation

The tool must apply the Princípio da Mínima Fatoração Necessária as a research hypothesis and dogfood rule:

> decompose only until further splitting no longer materially changes realization, sourcing, composition, exclusivity, risk or evidence-confidence decisions.

PMFN is not yet scientifically validated and must be presented as an experimental protocol hypothesis.

## Investigation order

```text
UNDERSTAND INTENT
  ↓
EXTRACT CLAIMS / ASSUMPTIONS
  ↓
FACTOR ONLY AS NEEDED
  ↓
SEARCH FOR EXISTING COVERAGE
  ↓
SEARCH FOR ADOPTION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR CONFIGURATION / EXTENSION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR COMPOSITION / RECONFIGURATION
  ↓
SEARCH FOR ADAPTATION
  ↓
IDENTIFY RESIDUAL FACTORIZATION
  ↓
CHARACTERIZE EXCLUSIVITY
  ↓
CHARACTERIZE EVIDENCE STRENGTH / SEARCH COVERAGE
  ↓
PROJECT EVIDENCE POSITION
  ↓
HUMAN DECISION
```

## Position dimensions

At minimum, the protocol should be able to represent:

```text
P   = observed pertinence
EC  = existing coverage
XC  = conceptual exclusivity
XA  = architectural exclusivity
XE  = execution exclusivity
FR  = residual factorization
EM  = evidence maturity
RC  = research/search coverage
```

No aggregate idea grade is required.

All numeric scales remain experimental until construct validity, calibration and reliability are established.

## Realization model

Do not force every result into a single ordinal scale. At minimum distinguish:

```text
provenance:
  existing | modified | invented | unknown

transformation:
  none | configure | extend | adapt | unknown

architecture:
  direct | compose | reconfigure | unknown

source:
  OSS | COTS | internal | outsourced | service | standard | unknown
```

Composition may itself be the locus of differentiation and must not be interpreted as absence of innovation.

## Required evidence

At minimum, when applicable:

- raw idea and evolving boundary;
- explicit assumptions;
- candidate universe;
- complete-product alternatives;
- partial capability alternatives;
- composition/reconfiguration alternatives;
- configuration and extension mechanisms;
- adaptation options;
- technical feasibility;
- maintenance burden;
- sustainability constraints;
- licensing and operational risks;
- market evidence when commercial relevance is characterized;
- academic prior art when scientific relevance is characterized;
- residual-factorization definition;
- uncertainty and blind spots;
- evidence maturity;
- search coverage;
- counterevidence;
- provenance.

## Evidence before metric

Every metric or categorical conclusion must be traceable to its evidence and uncertainty.

For example, high observed conceptual exclusivity with low search coverage must be presented as a weakly supported observation, never as proof of novelty.

## Separation of dimensions

Engineering, business, research and human alignment must remain distinguishable.

Commercial relevance does not establish academic novelty.
Academic novelty does not establish business value.
Technical feasibility does not justify proprietary ownership when adoption dominates.
World evidence does not encode the human's values or objectives.

## Human decision outputs

After reading the evidence position, a human may choose among actions such as:

- continue;
- reformulate;
- investigate more;
- adopt an existing solution;
- configure/extend/adapt/compose;
- defer;
- stop;
- proceed to project identity.

These are human decisions, not protocol verdicts.

## Completion

Naming is allowed only after the proposal is sufficiently stabilized for identity work and the human explicitly chooses to continue.
