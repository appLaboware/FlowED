# Project Genesis — Changelog

## 2026-09-07

### Added

- institutional pre-consultation model preserving human judgment;
- separation between world evidence and human alignment;
- future MyTrues role as durable alignment/context source rather than replacement for external evidence;
- Project Evidence Dossier contract;
- multidimensional project-position fingerprint;
- residual factorization (`FR`) terminology;
- evidence maturity and research/search coverage as first-class dimensions;
- temporal/versioned assessment semantics;
- consultant brief projection;
- self-dogfood assessment seed;
- InitProj seed dogfood assessment seed.

### Changed

- Project Genesis is now explicitly positioned as an evidence-positioning and project-birth system rather than an idea judge;
- `viability-gate` semantics were broadened from GO/NO-GO verdicting to neutral evidence positioning, sourcing discovery, exclusivity characterization and residual-factorization analysis;
- human decisions such as continue, reformulate, adopt, defer or stop are no longer protocol verdicts;
- composition is modeled as potentially transversal to provenance/transformation rather than necessarily one ordinal step in a reuse ladder.

### Research linkage

- PMFN remains an unvalidated research hypothesis;
- Project Genesis is designated as a candidate reference implementation and dogfood environment for PMFN;
- institutional pre-consultation introduces a candidate research question about whether evidence-informed AI preparation reallocates human consultation time toward higher-value judgment.
