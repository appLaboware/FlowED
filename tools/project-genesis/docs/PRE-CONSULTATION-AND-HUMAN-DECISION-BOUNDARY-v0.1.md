# Project Genesis — Pre-Consultation and Human Decision Boundary

Status: `INCUBATION / DOGFOODING`

## Purpose

Define Project Genesis as an evidence-informed pre-consultation instrument that prepares a human for a higher-value consultation rather than replacing human judgment.

## Founding position

> **The AI does not decide for the human. It lets the idea confront available evidence before the human decides.**

The system must not label an idea as good, bad, approved, rejected, promising or doomed merely from a composite score.

It produces a position, a traceable evidence case, unresolved questions, and an explicit boundary showing where human judgment remains necessary.

## Institutional use case

A support organization, incubator, university, accelerator, consultancy or public entrepreneurship service may use the tool before a human session.

```text
CANDIDATE
    ↓
structured conversation
    ↓
idea clarification
assumptions
capability factorization
search for existing solutions
reuse / adaptation / composition analysis
evidence quality and search coverage
uncertainties
idea evolution
    ↓
PRE-CONSULTATION DOSSIER
    ├─────────────→ candidate
    └─────────────→ consultant
                         ↓
                  HUMAN CONSULTATION
```

The machine compresses searchable and repetitive work. Human time is reserved for interpretation, tacit experience, trade-offs, values, responsibility and judgment.

## Neutrality rule

The protocol may surface evidence that causes a person to continue, reformulate, adopt an existing solution, change the business model, postpone, investigate further or abandon the idea.

These are human decisions.

The system may say:

- what evidence was found;
- what assumptions conflict with evidence;
- what alternatives exist;
- what remains uncertain;
- what evidence is weak;
- what decisions still require a human.

The system must not convert those observations into a hidden prescriptive verdict.

## World evidence versus human alignment

These must remain separate:

```text
WORLD EVIDENCE
what exists?
what has been observed?
what alternatives are available?
what is common or uncommon?
what evidence supports each claim?

HUMAN ALIGNMENT
what matters to this person or institution?
what constraints exist?
what objectives are preferred?
what risks are acceptable?
what must remain owned?
```

A future MyTrues integration may provide durable human/project/institutional context, but it must not alter factual evidence about the external world. It contributes alignment context, not epistemic substitution.

## Four complementary intelligences

1. **WORLD** — external evidence and prior art.
2. **PROTOCOL** — how the proposal is decomposed, investigated and characterized.
3. **MEMORY / MYTRUES** — durable context about the human, project or institution.
4. **HUMAN** — responsibility, values, interpretation and decision.

These sources must remain provenance-distinguishable.

## Idea evolution is evidence

A project proposal is expected to change during investigation.

```text
IDEA v0
  ↓ evidence
IDEA v1
  ↓ evidence
IDEA v2
  ↓ human alignment
IDEA v3
```

The system must preserve this genealogy. A changed idea is not a failed run; it is part of the evidence produced by the run.

## Consultant boundary artifact

Every institutional pre-consultation should be able to produce a short consultant brief containing:

- original idea;
- current idea;
- material changes made by the candidate;
- strongest evidence;
- strongest counterevidence;
- alternatives already investigated;
- decisions already made by the candidate;
- unresolved assumptions;
- low-confidence conclusions;
- questions that genuinely require human judgment;
- provenance links to the complete dossier.

## Research hypothesis enabled by this use case

Candidate research question:

> Does evidence-informed AI pre-consultation increase the proportion of human consultation time dedicated to high-value judgment while preserving or improving decision quality?

This is an unvalidated hypothesis and must not be stated as a proven efficiency gain.
