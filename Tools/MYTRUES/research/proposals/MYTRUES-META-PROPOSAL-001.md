## Proposed future demand

The package recommends:

`MYTRUES-MICROBRAIN-V0 — Controlled Cognitive Memory Experiment`

Its purpose is an experimental instrument, not production MyTrues.

Proposed minimum surface includes:

- controlled memory schema and fixtures;
- deterministic Systematic Agent;
- deterministic Gold Evaluator;
- experiment configuration registry;
- repeatable reset/replay;
- machine-readable run/evidence logging;
- no pretrained LLM factual knowledge required to compute gold answers.

Proposed acceptance includes deterministic repeatability, at least 50 gold
questions, at least 5 configurations, inspectable configuration/result
differences, measured contextual/historical errors, evidence paths and clean
reruns.

Candidate first switches are context, temporal handling, supersession, defeaters
and transitive relation.
