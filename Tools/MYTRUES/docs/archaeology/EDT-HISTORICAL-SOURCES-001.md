## 1. January 2025 prototype

`prototipo_01.pdf` is a January-2025 UTFPR/PPGI proposal.

It explicitly uses:

**Education-Driven Thinking (EDT)**

and describes EDT as a **conceptual paradigm**.

Core historical statements include:

- the creator cognitive process should be recorded and structured;
- this structured cognitive documentation should become a foundation for later
  documentation/knowledge evolution;
- the work investigates feasibility in software engineering;
- the research method can initially rely on theoretical argumentation,
  systematic literature review and comparison rather than immediate direct
  empirical experimentation.

Historical caution:

the document belongs to an earlier academic stage and includes a motivation for
a master's/PPGI context.

Current researcher intent is doctoral formation.

Do not rewrite the historical artifact; preserve the transition.

## 2. Article roadmap

`roadmap_artigo_opiniao.docx` contains the conceptual article roadmap.

Especially important:

- central question: what if creator cognition/path became the base for the
  documentation-production chain and normative knowledge evolution?
- the author explicitly describes the work as a **conceptual, philosophical
  thesis**, not primarily a practical/technological thesis;
- the document treats implementation/automation as a separate supporting line;
- it explicitly distinguishes proposing the paradigm from later testing it.

This is strong evidence that EDT's identity is conceptual rather than a software
architecture.

## 4. Datasheets pack

`datasheets.zip` contains historical conceptual datasheets, including:

- `datasheets/CCW/CCW.md`;
- CGT/TGC material;
- TCA;
- other early cognitive taxonomy work.

### CCW

`Creator Cognitive Walkthrough (CCW)` is especially important.

It proposes:

- capture of the creator cognitive process;
- progressive/rastreable documentation;
- preservation of errors, discarded hypotheses and difficulties;
- **cognitive keyframes** as selected critical moments.

This is a direct precursor to the later CCP concept.

Do not collapse:

`CCW == CCP`.

Preserve:

`CCW -> historical conceptual precursor of CCP`.

### TCA / CGT

These documents explore how cognition originates and is mediated.

They may inform EDT theory.

They do not define MyTrues runtime ontology.

## 6. Safe conclusions

The source pack safely supports:

### EDT

`EDT = Education-Driven Thinking`.

EDT is historically conceptual/philosophical in orientation.

The creator cognitive process/path is central.

### CCP genealogy

A creator-cognitive-path line clearly predates MyTrues as current protocol/tool.

CCW cognitive-keyframe work is part of that genealogy.

### MyTrues relationship

The historical source pack does not itself define the current MyTrues protocol.

Current project orientation adds MyTrues as the technical instrument/reference
implementation that can operationalize and test CCP-related hypotheses.

That relationship must be kept explicit:

```text
EDT thesis
-> CCP concept
-> MyTrues experimental/technical instrument
```

not:

```text
MyTrues architecture
-> defines EDT thesis
```

## 7. Claims that remain research questions

Do not treat historical working statements as settled science, including:

- uniqueness/novelty of EDT;
- claims that prior art does not address creator cognitive process;
- specific learning-effect claims;
- industry-impact claims;
- claims about normative adoption or economic value;
- neuroscience analogies.

Those require the current systematic prior-art/science process.
