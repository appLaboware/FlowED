# EDT-001 Tree — Assessment for EDT Reinforcement

Date: 2026-10-01

Status: HISTORICAL / CONCEPTUAL REINFORCEMENT ASSESSMENT

## Executive conclusion

The EDT-001 tree materially reinforces the Education-Driven Thinking thesis when conceptual material is separated from speculative implementation.

Strong reinforcement:
- creator cognition is treated as a primary object rather than only the final consolidated artifact;
- raw source, extraction, inference, confirmation, evidence and projection are distinct epistemic states;
- provenance and rationale are separate but linked concerns;
- rejected alternatives and failures are valuable parts of the cognitive path;
- projections should remain grounded in sources and must not become authority merely because they are readable;
- technical tooling can operationalize/test the thesis without defining the thesis.

## Conceptual core

Historical EDT.md states that the cognition that generated an artifact is more valuable, replicable and educational than the final artifact alone, and that the creation process should be documented and treated as a primary object.

This supports the mature formulation: consolidated artifact + CCP, not CCP instead of the consolidated artifact.

## Epistemic chain

The Level-0 material proposes a strong invariant:

SOURCE != EXTRACTED != INFERRED != CONFIRMED != EVIDENCED != PROJECTION

No derived object may silently upgrade its epistemic status.

This is highly relevant to CCP because an AI-generated summary, graph or projection must never be confused with the original creator cognition.

## Rejected paths

The material treats rejected candidates, failed paths and superseded knowledge as assets. This reinforces the thesis hypothesis that understanding why something was rejected can matter as much as the final accepted result.

## Useful three-layer model

SOURCE -> CCP -> PROJECTION

SOURCE: what was actually said, done or observed.
CCP: structured creator-cognitive path containing questions, alternatives, rationale, evidence, decisions, rejected paths and revisions.
PROJECTION: NORM, ADR, explanation, learning material, timeline or other derived view.

## Useful research propositions

P1 Consolidation loss: a final artifact may not preserve enough information to reconstruct the reasoning that produced it.
P2 Cognitive-path traceability: selected cognitive keyframes may improve later understanding of why an artifact evolved as it did.
P3 Rejected paths have epistemic value.
P4 Derived documentation is more trustworthy when source-grounded.
P5 Creator rationale may support critical/contextual adoption rather than mechanical compliance.
P6 CCP can preserve selected explicit cognitive keyframes without requiring exhaustive internal thought capture.

## Material not to promote into EDT

Do not treat the following as thesis identity or established science:
- Education-Driven Things;
- Experiential Decision Theory;
- digital-neuron / synaptic-weight / trauma-marker metaphors;
- federated experience marketplace;
- automatic experiential weights;
- graph/vector architecture as EDT itself;
- disappearance of the consolidated document in favor of only dynamic consolidation.

These belong to historical or assistant-generated implementation/product speculation.
