# EDT, CCP and MyTrues — Research Boundary

Date: 2026-10-01

Status: **CURRENT RESEARCH BOUNDARY / PROJECT ORIENTATION**

## Purpose

Prevent three different things from being collapsed into one:

- **EDT** — the conceptual/philosophical research thesis;
- **CCP** — the central object/concept under investigation;
- **MyTrues** — a technical instrument/reference tool used to operationalize and
  test parts of that conceptual proposal.

This boundary supersedes archaeology shorthand that treated EDT as a technology,
runtime protocol, product, or alternative implementation of MyTrues.

## 1. EDT

Current research intent:

**Education-Driven Thinking (EDT)** is a conceptual doctoral research thesis in
engineering, with a philosophical/epistemological character.

Its central concern is not to invent a software architecture.

It asks what changes if the **creator's cognitive path** is preserved and treated
as a primary source for understanding, documentation, learning, normative
knowledge and evolution, rather than preserving only the consolidated artifact.

Historical source material already supports this conceptual orientation.

A January-2025 proposal described EDT as:

- a **conceptual paradigm**;
- centered on the creator's cognitive process;
- intended to structure documentation and traceability from that process;
- methodologically supportable by theoretical argumentation and systematic
  literature review, without requiring immediate direct experimental proof.

The current academic stage is doctoral formation; the January-2025 material is
historical evidence from an earlier academic stage and must not be rewritten as
if it had already been a doctoral document.

## 2. CCP

**CCP — Caminho Cognitivo do Criador / Creator Cognitive Path** is the central
conceptual object that EDT seeks to investigate and defend.

Working intuition:

```text
creator encounters/creates a problem
-> questions
-> hypotheses
-> alternatives
-> arguments / counterarguments
-> evidence / experience
-> decisions
-> errors / revisions
-> resulting artifact / norm / knowledge
```

The claim under study is not that every internal thought must be captured.

Historical CCW material already proposed **cognitive keyframes**: selected
critical moments of decision/reflection that preserve enough of the path to make
the creation understandable and reconstructable.

Therefore:

`CCP != raw chain-of-thought dump`.

The current project must continue to prefer explicit, preservable cognition,
rationale, evidence, decisions and provenance rather than hidden internal
reasoning.

## 3. MyTrues

**MyTrues is not EDT.**

**MyTrues is not the thesis.**

MyTrues is a technical tool/reference implementation that can make CCP-related
ideas operational enough to inspect, query, reconstruct and test.

Its role relative to the thesis is approximately:

```text
EDT
conceptual/philosophical thesis
        ↓
CCP
central concept / object of investigation
        ↓
MyTrues
technical instrument / reference implementation
        ↓
observable cases, queries, reconstructions, conformance,
experiments and evidence about what CCP enables
```

MyTrues can therefore provide **indirect empirical support / feasibility
evidence** for the value and operational consequences of preserving CCP.

But failure or success of any particular MyTrues implementation does not, by
itself, prove or disprove the whole EDT thesis.

Likewise, EDT must not be retroactively defined by whatever storage, graph,
protocol, engine or adapter happens to be used by MyTrues.

## 4. Separate novelty questions

The project must maintain two independent novelty questions.

### 4.1 EDT / CCP scientific question

Potential contribution is conceptual.

Examples of questions:

- Is the creator cognitive path a useful primary epistemic/documentation object?
- What is lost when only the consolidated artifact is preserved?
- Can normative/technical knowledge be better understood or evolved when the
  path that generated it remains inspectable?
- What should count as a sufficient CCP representation?
- How does CCP relate to design rationale, traceability, provenance,
  organizational memory, experiential learning and knowledge evolution?

This line belongs to the thesis.

### 4.2 MyTrues technical/scientific question

Potential contributions may arise while building the instrument.

Examples:

- data/protocol representations;
- temporal and provenance semantics;
- decision-memory behavior;
- human authority boundaries;
- portable cross-agent memory;
- deterministic reconstruction;
- conformance;
- retrieval versus epistemic authority;
- decision/profile mechanisms;
- experiments showing measurable value.

These may produce papers, tools or product opportunities.

They are **secondary research opportunities**, not prerequisites for EDT to
remain a valid conceptual thesis.

## 5. Adopt-first consequence

A critical consequence follows:

**MyTrues does not need technically novel components in order to be useful to
EDT.**

If the best implementation is:

```text
existing open tools
+ configuration
+ composition
+ adapters
+ conformance
```

that can still be a perfectly valid experimental instrument for CCP.

Therefore:

`technical reuse != scientific failure of EDT`.

The Science Frontier gate for MyTrues must not be misused as a gate on the
existence/value of the EDT thesis.

## 6. Historical evidence

### January 2025 — EDT prototype

The preserved proposal already named:

`Education-Driven Thinking (EDT)`

and described the creator cognitive process as the structuring basis of
documentation and knowledge evolution.

It explicitly framed EDT as a **conceptual paradigm**.

Its methodology proposed theoretical argumentation and systematic literature
review and stated that immediate direct empirical experimentation was not
required for the initial conceptual proposal.

### February 2025 — CCW precursor

`Creator Cognitive Walkthrough (CCW)` proposed:

- capturing the creator cognitive process;
- preserving the reasoning behind decisions;
- recording errors, discarded hypotheses and difficulties;
- using **cognitive keyframes** rather than only a final state.

This is a direct conceptual ancestor of the later CCP line.

CCW should be preserved as historical genealogy, not silently renamed as if it
had always been CCP.

### February 2025 — CGT / cognitive-origin work

The CGT/TCA material explored classification by:

- source/origin of cognition;
- production versus reconstruction;
- pure versus consolidated representation;
- mediation.

This belongs to the conceptual research genealogy around cognition and EDT.

It is not automatically part of current MyTrues protocol semantics.

## 7. Naming

For current research orientation:

- **EDT = Education-Driven Thinking**.
- **CCP = Caminho Cognitivo do Criador**.
- English working form: **Creator Cognitive Path**.
- **CCW = Creator Cognitive Walkthrough** is a historical precursor/model, not
  the current expansion of CCP.

The archaeology conflict around `Education-Driven Things` must therefore be
treated as non-canonical unless future primary evidence shows an explicit later
rename by the human researcher.

## 8. Research discipline

Do not use MyTrues implementation choices to manufacture EDT novelty.

Do not use EDT's conceptual ambitions to justify unnecessary MyTrues invention.

Maintain:

```text
EDT/CCP prior-art review
        independent of
MyTrues technical prior-art review
```

Then connect them experimentally:

```text
CCP proposition
-> operationalization through MyTrues
-> observable experiment/use
-> evidence
-> interpretation back into EDT research
```

## 9. Current north

The current relationship is:

```text
EDT
= conceptual/philosophical engineering thesis

CCP
= central creator-cognitive-path concept

MyTrues
= technical instrument used to operationalize/test CCP
  + independent product/protocol opportunity
  + possible source of additional technical/scientific publications
```

This relationship should guide future archaeology, science-frontier research and
product architecture.


## Additional reinforcement from EDT-001 tree

A later historical archive reinforces the thesis/tool boundary and adds a useful epistemic decomposition.

Strong conceptual reinforcement:

- creator cognition as a primary object;
- the final consolidated artifact as consequence/projection rather than the complete epistemic object;
- source, extraction, inference, confirmation, evidence and projection as distinct states;
- provenance distinct from rationale;
- rejected alternatives and failed paths as useful parts of the creator cognitive path;
- projection non-authority: a readable derived document does not replace its source.

A useful theoretical model emerging from this material is:

SOURCE -> CCP -> PROJECTION

where:

- SOURCE is what was actually said, done or observed;
- CCP is the structured creator-cognitive path;
- PROJECTION is a derived NORM/ADR/explanation/learning/timeline view.

This does not make that pipeline the only valid CCP representation.

The archive also contains speculative product/implementation material that is explicitly excluded from thesis identity, including neural metaphors, experiential synaptic weights, federated marketplaces and alternate EDT expansions.
