## Role after reorganization

These texts are preserved for:

- academic/historical provenance;
- CCP terminology and evolution;
- future comparison against the generic MyTrues decision protocol;
- construction of CCP Records.

They are **not automatically normative runtime specification**.

Where historical text conflicts with the current generic protocol,
`docs/GENERIC-PROTOCOL.md`, the executable v0.2 schemas/conformance, and later
versioned canonical specifications take precedence.

Claims in historical research/vision documents remain historical claims until
independently revalidated.
