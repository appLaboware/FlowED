# MyTrues Evolution - Refined Knowledge

## 📋 Summary

MyTrues evolved from a simple idea about AI training limitations into a comprehensive protocol for experiential knowledge management and social intelligence.

## 🎯 Context

**Problem**: Current AI systems train on consolidated data (Wikipedia, papers, books) but lose the cognitive journey that created those conclusions.

**Opportunity**: Capture Creator's Cognitive Path (CCP) to preserve experiential knowledge and enable better AI training.

**Setting**: Real-time collaborative session between human intuition and AI assistance (June 22, 2025).

## 🔄 Solution Evolution

### Phase 1: Knowledge Management Tool
- **Initial concept**: Simple CCC (Context, Change, Consequence) framework
- **Target**: Organizational decision documentation
- **CLI**: Basic CRUD operations for "truths"

### Phase 2: Neural Network of Knowledge
- **Breakthrough insight**: "Sinapses between truths"
- **Innovation**: Emotional/experiential connections between knowledge nodes
- **Types**: Causal, motivational, temporal, contextual connections

### Phase 3: Experiential Decision Theory (EDT)
- **Philosophical foundation**: Experience > Consolidated knowledge
- **Dynamic consolidation**: Real-time knowledge generation vs. static docs
- **AI revolution**: Train models on cognitive paths, not just conclusions

### Phase 4: Social Intelligence Platform
- **Global application**: Track opinion evolution, not just snapshots
- **Market impact**: Marketing, politics, education, social reconciliation
- **Civilizational scope**: Platform for collective human intelligence
